FAQ

House Cleaning Pricing Questions

How much should beginners charge for house cleaning?

Many beginners start between $120 and $350 depending on home size, cleaning condition, bathrooms, kitchens, add-ons, and local market pricing.


Should deep cleaning cost more?

Yes. Deep cleaning usually takes much longer and requires more effort, supplies, and labor time.


Do cleaning supplies affect pricing?

Absolutely. Supplies, fuel, travel, and equipment wear should all be included.
