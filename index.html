Pricing guide

How much should beginners charge for pressure washing?

Most beginner pressure washing prices depend on square footage, dirt level, job difficulty, chemical cost, fuel cost, travel time, and how much profit the owner wants to keep. A calculator helps you avoid random guessing by turning the job into a simple pricing formula.

What affects your price?

  • Job size: Larger jobs usually cost more but may have better efficiency.
  • Dirt level: Heavy buildup, oil, algae, and stains can require more time and chemicals.
  • Travel: Fuel and distance should be included so the job does not eat your profit.
  • Setup time: Loading, unloading, hose setup, rinsing, cleanup, and customer communication all take time.
  • Minimum fee: Many service businesses need a minimum job price to make small jobs worth taking.

Important: This tool is a beginner-friendly estimate, not a guaranteed market price. Your final quote should reflect your local market, experience, equipment, safety risk, and the real condition of the job.
